# Review: types, delays, appeals

Every version of a PlayerVox OverCrow widget, and every change of its
listing, is read by a person at PlayerVox before players get it. This page
covers the two kinds of review, their delays, the review thread and the
appeal. To send a version, see [publish a widget](publish-a-widget.md).

## Quick or full

| Review | When | Longest delay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full | The first publication, or an update that adds a permission, a network route or a capability | 5 business days |
| Quick | Any other update, or a change of the listing alone | 2 business days |
| Appeal | Once per decision, within six months | 7 days |
| Publication | After the approval | 24 business hours |

Business days run Monday to Friday, except French public holidays. The delay
is paused while the review waits for your reply or a corrected version.
A quick review reads the lines that changed since the last approved
version; a full review reads the whole widget.

## The review thread

Each version in review has a thread in the creator space:

- A **remark** points to a file and a line, quotes the code the reviewer
  read and cites a numbered rule of the publication rules.
- You can **reply** to the reviewer. Your reply sends the review back to
  the reviewer when it was waiting for you.
- A **corrected version** continues the same thread: send it from
  **Submit a version**, with a newer version number.
- The **decision** is approval, changes requested or a refusal, always
  with its reasons.

Reviewers are shown as "PlayerVox reviewer", never by name. Every time a
reviewer opens your sources, the creator space shows when, which version
and why, under **Access to your sources**.

## Appeal

You can contest a refusal or a suspension once per decision, within six
months, with **Contest the decision**. PlayerVox re-examines the decision
entirely and answers within 7 days, with its reasons. You can always send a
new version instead.

## Suspension

When players are at risk (malicious code, a leaked key, a data leak),
PlayerVox can suspend a widget or a version at once: installed copies are
turned off. The creator space gives the reason and the rule, and the
appeal stays open.

## Withdrawal

You can withdraw a version or the whole widget at any time: it leaves the
catalog at the next publication, and installed copies keep working. A
version in review or approved but not published yet is withdrawn at once.
